THE EXPERIMENT

How the agent learns

OVERVIEW
01
Observe

Position, altitude, linear velocity, tilt and angular velocity define the current state.

02
Act

The policy selects coast, rotation, thrust, or combined thrust-and-rotation actions.

03
Learn

Rewards update the Q-table. The learned controller is conservative and falls back to the heuristic when evidence is weak.
